# The Deep Ocean Trench

**Practice**: Engineering

---

## State Space

You are at **Earth's deepest point** — where the planet reaches its lowest.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Depth | Maximum (terrestrial) | 11 kilometers |
| Pressure | Maximum (oceanic) | 1,000+ atmospheres |
| Light | Zero | Perpetual darkness |

**Tension**: Low (inaccessible, stable).

---

## Action Set

In **Engineering** practice, your moves are exploration operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Model** | *speculate* | What lives there? | Unknown territory |
| **Deploy** | *prototype* | Bathyscaphe technology | Extreme engineering |
| **Abstract** | *widen* | All unexplored depths | Goes general |
| **Contemplate** | *suspend* | Imagining the crush | Philosophical |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Direct access* — human body cannot survive
- *Engage* — pressure lethal
