# The Deep Ocean Trench

## The Site

11 kilometers down. Pressure: 1,000 atmospheres — enough to crush submarines. No light has reached here for a billion years. Cold near freezing. Yet life exists: blind, strange, adapted to the impossible. The hadal zone — named for Hades, the underworld. The deepest places on Earth, less explored than the Moon.
