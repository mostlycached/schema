# The Body at Rest Before Movement

## The Site

You're standing still. Or sitting. Or lying. The body is at rest but not inert — every possible next movement is available. Raise a hand. Take a step. Turn your head. Breathe differently. The space of possible bodily actions opens outward in every direction.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: virtuality (potential kinesis)
scale: sub-momentary (the instant before) → extended (the movement to come)
temporal: anticipation (pre-movement)
perceptual: somatic (proprioceptive awareness)
relational: solitary (the body's self-relation) + environmental (body in space)
agential: anyone (embodied)
structural: composite (joints × muscles × intentions × space)
```

## Action Space Calculation

```
Joints:          ~70 (each with ~3 degrees of freedom)
Configurations:  ~200 dimensions of continuous variation
Qualities:       ~20 (each continuous)
Intentions:      ~50

Discretized:
- Positions: ~10 meaningful positions per joint
- Qualities: ~5 meaningful levels per quality
- Intentions: ~50

Minimal action space: 10^70 × 5^20 × 50 ≈ incomputably large

Meaningful action clusters:
- Gross motor movements: ~200 (step, reach, turn, bend...)
- Fine motor movements: ~500 (finger positions, facial expressions...)
- Movement qualities: ~100 (fast/slow, hard/soft combinations)
- Functional actions: ~300 (pick up, put down, open, close...)
- Expressive actions: ~1000 (gesture vocabulary, emotional expressions...)

Total meaningful discrete actions: ~2,100
```
