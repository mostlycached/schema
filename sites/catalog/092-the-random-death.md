# The Random Death

## The Site

They died. No reason. A stray bullet. A freak accident. Wrong place. Wrong time. Not illness that could be fought, not age that was expected, not danger that was chosen. Random. Arbitrary. The universe's roulette landed on them. They were alive, then dead, and there is no reason why.

## What's Revealed

When death is random:

1. **The universe is indifferent** — it doesn't care who lives or dies
2. **There is no pattern** — seeking one is projection
3. **It could have been anyone** — including you
4. **Death is arbitrary** — not earned, not deserved, not meaningful
5. **Life is arbitrary** — if death is random, so is survival

## The Cruelest Part

The cruelest part: **you're still looking for meaning**.

Even knowing it's random, you search. Why them? Why then? What does it mean? And the answer, always: nothing. It means nothing. They died. Randomly. That's the end of the sentence.
