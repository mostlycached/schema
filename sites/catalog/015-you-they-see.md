# The You They See

## The Site

You know who you are — or think you do. But when you catch yourself reflected in others' reactions, there's a stranger there. The You they see isn't the You you feel yourself to be.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: relation (the gap between self-model and other-model)
scale: extended
temporal: oscillation (appears in social moments)
perceptual: other-mediated
relational: dyadic → collective (depends on who's seeing)
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (same entity, different representations)
```

## Flux Relation

```

dissolution_resistance: high — the gap is structural
```
