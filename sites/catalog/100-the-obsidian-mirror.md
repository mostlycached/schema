# The Obsidian Mirror

**Practice**: Contemplation
**Constraint**: `temporal = result` AND `flow = 0` (The volcano *after*)

---

## State Space

You are at **the site of maximum cooling** — where flow became structure instantly.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Temperature | Cold | Heat memory only |
| Texture | Glass | Structure without crystal |
| Sharpness | Infinite | Edge to the molecule |
| History | Visible | The moment of freezing |

**Tension**: Low/High (Cold to touch, violent origin).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are reflection operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Touch** | *examine* | Feel the glass | Cut yourself |
| **Look** | *reflect* | See yourself in the black | Distortion |
| **Break** | *fragment* | Create new edges | Irreversible shatteredness |
| **Trace** | *reconstruct* | Follow flow lines backward | Vertigo of origin |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Flow* — movement is impossible now
- *Melt* — would require re-destruction
