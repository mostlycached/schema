# The Eye of the Storm

## The Site

A cylinder of stillness surrounded by violence. Winds of 300 km/h circle a core of calm sky. The eye can be 30-65 km across. Inside: clear air, sometimes visible sun, birds that got trapped. Outside: walls of rain moving at hurricane force. The paradox of peace within destruction — and the knowledge that you're only halfway through.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: paradoxical (calm within violence)
scale: mesoscale (tens of kilometers)
temporal: transitional (you're halfway, the storm continues)
perceptual: uncanny (peace surrounded by violence)
relational: isolated (cut off from the outside)
agential: observer (in the center)
structural: paradoxical (stillness that depends on motion)
```
