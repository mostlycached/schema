# The Conversation That Has Just Begun

## The Site

You and another person. Introductions are done. You're past "hello" but nowhere yet. The conversation is *open* — it could go anywhere. Every topic, every register, every depth is available. What do you say next?

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: relation (co-constructed)
scale: momentary → extended (could last minutes or hours)
temporal: transition (from opening to substance)
perceptual: direct + other-mediated (reading their cues)
relational: dyadic (the two of you create a temporary world)
agential: anyone
structural: composite (your moves + their responses + the emergent third)
```

---

```
available: [BRANCH, NARROW, COMMIT, DEFER, SUSPEND, WIDEN]

dominant:  BRANCH — keeping options open

ACTION SPACE NOTE: Action = your-choice × their-response × topic × register × depth
The space is the Cartesian product of multiple conversational dimensions.
```

## Action Space Calculation

```
Topics:        ~200
Register:      ~20
Depth:         ~10
Speech Act:    ~30
Turn:          ~15

Combinatorial space: 200 × 20 × 10 × 30 × 15 = 18,000,000 possible next moves

Constrained by:
- Context relevance: ÷100
- Social appropriateness: ÷10
- Their previous turn: ÷10

Effective meaningful action space: ~1,800 distinct conversational moves
```

## Response Anticipation Matrix

Your action space is modulated by anticipated response:

| Your Move | Likely Responses | Contingent Actions |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| Light topic | Light response, volley | Continue, deepen, shift |
| Deep question | Disclosure or deflection | Match depth or respect boundary |
| Challenge | Defense, agree, counter | Soften, persist, withdraw |
| Disclosure | Reciprocate or acknowledge | Deepen or stabilize |
| Silence | Fill or hold | Wait or speak |
