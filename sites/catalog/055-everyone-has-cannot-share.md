# The Experience Everyone Has But Cannot Share

**Practice**: Philosophy

---

## State Space

You are at **universal privacy** — everyone has it, no one can say it.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Universality | Maximum | Everyone has this |
| Sharability | Zero | Cannot communicate |
| Paradox | High | Shared unsharability |

**Tension**: Low-medium, existential. Together alone.

---

## Action Set

In **Philosophy** practice, your moves are communicative attempts:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Dwell** | *suspend* | Be with the unsharable | Isolation accepted |
| **Generalize** | *widen* | All such experiences | Scope increases |
| **Attempt** | *branch* | Try to say it — words, poetry, silence | Fails, but approaches |
| **Theorize** | *speculate* | Why can't we share? | Opens philosophy |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Define* — articulation fails
- *Complete* — communication blocked

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Accept and dwell
```
Experience → can't share → accept → be with it
```
**Payoff**: Isolation without struggle.

### Path B: Recognition
```
Experience → can't share → "you too?" → someone nods → together in isolation
```
**Payoff**: Connection without communication.

### Path C: Indirect evocation
```
Experience → can't share → try poetry, art → approach but don't capture
```
**Payoff**: Artistic compensation; still fails as transmission.

### Path D: Theorize
```
Experience → can't share → "why?" → qualia, private language → philosophy
```
**Payoff**: Understanding the structure of the limit.
