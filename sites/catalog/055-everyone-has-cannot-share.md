# The Experience Everyone Has But Cannot Share

## The Site

You've had it. I've had it. We've all had it. But when you try to say it — even to yourself — it dissolves or distorts. The experience of almost grasping something. The feeling just before sleep. The way your name sounds strange when repeated. The sense that you've lived before. Universal but incommunicable.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (communicability)
scale: momentary (the experience) + lifetime (its recurrence)
temporal: recurring (happens to everyone, repeatedly)
perceptual: direct (perfectly clear when happening) + absent (vanishes in description)
relational: universal (everyone) + solitary (no sharing)
agential: anyone (human condition)
structural: paradoxical (shared unsharability)
```
