# The Pain That Cannot Be Shown

## The Site

It hurts. You know it hurts. But when asked, you can't point to it, can't describe it, can't make it visible. The pain is real and utterly private. Others might believe you, but they can't *access* it.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: substance (but immaterial)
scale: variable
temporal: extended
perceptual: somatic (exclusively)
relational: solitary (even in company)
agential: anyone
structural: simple (but impenetrable)
```

## Flux Relation

```
flux_state: structured (the privacy is absolute)
dissolution_resistance: infinite for privacy; variable for pain
hover_potential: medium — can dwell in the isolation
emergence_pattern: communicative (trying to show reveals impossibility)
flux_pockets: what the pain "is like" remains flux to others
```

The privacy is not a flux state — it's a structural constraint. Within that structure, the pain itself may be flux (changing, uncertain) or structured (stable, chronic). The isolation is invariant.
