# The Boundary That Belongs to Neither Side

## The Site

The border between countries — where exactly is it? The line on the map has no width. The fence? The fence is a thing, not a boundary. The edge between day and night — does twilight belong to day or night? The skin between self and world — where does the self end? The boundary exists but has no territory of its own.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (pure edge)
scale: zero (the boundary has no width)
temporal: extended (persists as division)
perceptual: direct (you see the division) + invisible (you can't see the boundary itself)
relational: between (belongs to neither, defines both)
agential: varies (natural or constructed)
structural: boundary-only (exists only as separation)
```
