# The Cryovolcano

**Practice**: Physics
**Speculation**: Invert Properties (Heat → Cold, Rock → Ice)

---

## State Space

You are at **the thermodynamic inversion** — where cold erupts as a force.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Temperature | Near 0K | Absolute stillness as projectile |
| Phase | Super-solid | Ice harder than stone |
| Energy | Entropy | Eruption of order/stasis |
| Scale | Planetary | The frozen core expelling cold |

**Tension**: Paradoxical (Explosion of stillness).

---

## Action Set

In **Physics** practice, your moves are thermodynamic operations in reverse:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Approach** | *approach* | Investigate the flow | Freezing (cessation of action) |
| **Sample** | *narrow* | Collect the ejecta | Shatters on touch |
| **Measure** | *witness* | observe the stasis | Observer effect warms it |
| **Thermostat** | *block* | Attempt to heat | Futile; heat is consumed |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Ignite* — fire is impossible here
- *Melt* — state is too stable
