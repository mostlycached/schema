# The Life You Almost Lived

## The Site

There was a fork. You went one way. The other path didn't disappear — it became a ghost. Sometimes you can almost see it: the person you would have been, the rooms you would have lived in, the problems you would have had instead.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: virtuality
scale: lifetime
temporal: memory + anticipation (past fork, ongoing haunting)
perceptual: peripheral (glimpsed, not directly seen)
relational: solitary (the other you is alone in their world)
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (real enough to mourn, never existed)
```
