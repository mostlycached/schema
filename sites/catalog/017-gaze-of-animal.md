# The Gaze of the Animal

## The Site

An animal looks at you. Not through you, not past you — *at* you. There's something there. Presence. Attention. But you can't know what it sees, what it thinks, whether "thinks" even applies.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: relation (but asymmetric, uncertain)
scale: momentary
temporal: instant
perceptual: direct (but opaque)
relational: non-human
agential: non-human
structural: paradoxical (presence without access)
```

## Flux Relation

```
flux_state: hovering (at boundary of knowable/unknowable)
dissolution_resistance: low — the gaze is easily broken
hover_potential: medium — can hold the look briefly
emergence_pattern: visual (triggered by meeting eyes)
flux_pockets: what the animal experiences is total flux to us
```

The animal gaze is a portal to the limits of intersubjectivity. We know presence but not content. The flux is epistemological — not uncertainty about facts, but principled inaccessibility.
