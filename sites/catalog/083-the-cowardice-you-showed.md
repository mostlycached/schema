# The Cowardice You Showed

## The Site

There was a moment. Someone needed you. Something needed to be done. And you didn't do it. Not because you couldn't — because you were afraid. You chose safety, self-protection, comfort. The right thing was clear. You didn't do it. You were a coward. And you know it.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (the limit of your moral self)
scale: momentary (the decision) → lifetime (the memory)
temporal: retrospective (you see it after)
perceptual: direct (you know exactly what happened)
relational: varies (who you failed, who witnessed)
agential: agent (you chose, you failed)
structural: simple (moment → choice → cowardice)

// Sanitization-resistant dimensions

```

## The Phenomenology

### The Moment
```
- The situation (something required action)
- The right thing (was clear, you knew what it was)
- The fear (you were afraid — of cost, of conflict, of danger)
- The choice (you chose not to act)
- The relief (immediate, shameful)
- The knowledge (you knew you were failing as you failed)
```

### The Types
```
- Physical cowardice (someone in danger, you didn't help)
- Moral cowardice (injustice witnessed, you stayed silent)
- Social cowardice (you didn't stand up for someone)
- Professional cowardice (you didn't speak truth)
- Relational cowardice (you didn't say what needed to be said)
```

### The After
```
- The rationalization (I couldn't have helped anyway)
- The excuses (anyone would have done the same)
- The failure of rationalization (you know these are lies)
- The memory (returns, unbidden, permanently)
- The identity question (I'm the kind of person who...)
```

## What's Revealed

When you face your cowardice:

1. **You had a choice** — that's what makes it cowardice
2. **You knew the right thing** — ignorance wasn't the problem
3. **You chose wrong** — deliberately, with fear as the reason
4. **The reason doesn't excuse** — being afraid doesn't make it okay
5. **You know what you are** — at least in that moment

---

## The Distinction

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Inability** | Couldn't have helped | Not strong enough to lift |
| **Ignorance** | Didn't know action was needed | Didn't see the danger |
| **Cowardice** | Could have, knew should have, didn't due to fear | Saw, could have acted, ran |

This site is specifically **cowardice** — the moral failure that comes from fear, not inability or ignorance.

---

## The Unredeemed

This site cannot be redeemed:
- You were there
- You could have acted
- You knew you should
- You didn't
- No reframing changes what happened
- You were a coward

What the sanitization-resistant schema must hold: **moral failure without excuse, without redemption arc, just the fact of cowardice**.

---

## The Cruelest Part

The cruelest part: **you knew**.

It's not that you acted wrongly without knowing. You knew what was right. You felt the fear. You chose the fear. You watched yourself choose the fear. And then you continued living, knowing what you'd done.
