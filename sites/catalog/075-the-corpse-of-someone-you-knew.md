# The Corpse of Someone You Knew

**Practice**: Contemplation

---

## State Space

You are at **the personhood limit** — presence of body, absence of subject.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Ontological | Paradox | Look-like vs Is-not |
| Agency | Zero | The corpse does nothing |
| Affect | Abject | Horror/Fascination |
| Future | Rot | Immediate decay |

**Tension**: Maximum (The Uncanny Valley).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are limit-witnessing:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Stare** | *witness* | Confront the gap | Nausea |
| **Touch** | *commit* | Cold/Waxen feel | Trauma |
| **Recoil** | *branch* | Look away | Denial |
| **Project** | *widen* | "I will be this" | Existential vertigo |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Connect* — no one there
- *Awaken* — impossible
- *Save* — too late

## Strategy Space

### Path A: The Sanitized
```
Look -> "They're sleeping" -> "Better place" -> Move on
```
**Payoff**: Emotional protection; denial of the abject.

### Path B: The Confrontation
```
Look -> See the absence -> Accept the thing-ness -> "Gone"
```
**Payoff**: Radical acceptance of death's reality.

### Path C: The Projection
```
Look -> See myself -> "I am meat waiting to happen"
```
**Payoff**: Memento mori; existential clarity.
