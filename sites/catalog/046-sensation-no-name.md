# The Sensation With No Name

## The Site

You feel it. Distinctly. But there's no word for it. Not a mix of known feelings — something else entirely. The Germans might have a word. The Japanese might. But in your language, this sensation has no name. It exists in the gap between vocabulary and experience.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (lexical gap)
scale: momentary to extended
temporal: present (felt now)
perceptual: somatic (distinctly felt) + absent (from lexicon)
relational: varies (might be shared, can't be named)
agential: anyone (in any language with gaps)
structural: incomplete (the vocabulary has holes)
```
