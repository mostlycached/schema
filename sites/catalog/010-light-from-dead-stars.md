# The Light That Left Before You Were Born

## The Site

You look up. The stars are bright. But the light you see left its source years, centuries, millennia ago. Some of those stars are already dead. You're seeing ghosts that don't know they're ghosts.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: absence (disguised as presence)
scale: cosmic
temporal: trans-lifetime (the journey took longer than lives)
perceptual: direct (deceptively so)
relational: non-human (the stars don't know you're watching)
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (present light, absent source)
```
