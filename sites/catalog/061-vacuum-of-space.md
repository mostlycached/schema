# The Vacuum of Space

## The Site

Between the stars: almost nothing. One atom per cubic centimeter in interstellar space. In intergalactic space: one atom per cubic meter. Temperature: 2.7 Kelvin — just above absolute zero, the faint warmth of the Big Bang's afterglow. No up, no down. No atmosphere to carry sound. The closest thing to nothing that exists.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: absence (near-total)
scale: cosmic (fills most of the universe)
temporal: eternal (has existed since Big Bang)
perceptual: absent (no sensation in vacuum)
relational: none (too far from everything)
agential: none (nothing lives here)
structural: simple (emptiness, uniformity)
```
