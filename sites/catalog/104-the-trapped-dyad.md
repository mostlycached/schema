# The Trapped Dyad

**Practice**: Sociology
**Base**: `dyadic` + `danger` (Volcano)
**Layer 1**: `structural = enclosed` (Confined Space)

---

## State Space

You are **sealed in the view** — the danger is outside, but the pressure is inside.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Space | Confined | 5x5 bunker/pod |
| Relation | Inescapable | Cannot leave connection |
| External | Hostile | Lava/Ash outside glass |
| Internal | Claustrophobic | Breath, sweat, gaze |

**Tension**: High (Ambient danger + Social friction).

---

## Action Set

In **Sociology** practice, moves are intensified by enclosure:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Pace** | *channel* | Physical release in small space | Irritates other |
| **Project** | *transfer* | Blame other for situation | Conflict in box |
| **Huddle** | *connect* | Physical comfort | Overheating |
| **Watch** | *witness* | Look out the viewport | Dissociation from dyad |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Leave* — blocked by shell
- *Distance* — blocked by walls

## Strategy Space

### Path A: The Pressure Cooker
```
Trapped → annoyance builds → no escape → explosion (social) matching outside (physical)
```
**Payoff**: Catharsis; destruction of relationship.

### Path B: The Hibernation
```
Trapped → conserve energy → slow down → sync breathing → become one organism
```
**Payoff**: Survival; loss of individuation.
