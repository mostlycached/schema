# The Earthquake Fault Line

**Practice**: Engineering

---

## State Space

You are on **the locked fault** — stored energy waiting for release.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Stored energy | Enormous | Decades/centuries of stress |
| Predictability | Zero | Cannot predict timing |
| Consequence | Catastrophic | Cities, landscapes reshaped |

**Tension**: Accumulating (constantly increasing).

---

## Action Set

In **Engineering** practice, your moves are limited by uncertainty:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Dwell** | *suspend* | Living on the fault | Invisible stress |
| **Predict** | *speculate* | When will it release? | Impossible |
| **Abstract** | *widen* | All fault lines | Goes geological |
| **Hope** | *defer* | Maybe not in my lifetime | Denial |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Safe release* — tension cannot be safely discharged
- *Resolve* — only earthquake resolves

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Live with it
```
Fault → stress accumulating → accept uncertainty → dwell
```
**Payoff**: Awareness without resolution.

### Path B: Defer
```
Fault → stress accumulating → "maybe not my lifetime" → hope
```
**Payoff**: Psychological comfort; may be wrong.

### Path C: Prepare
```
Fault → stress accumulating → earthquake engineering → mitigation
```
**Payoff**: Can't prevent; can reduce damage.
