# The Earthquake Fault Line

## The Site

A crack in the Earth's crust where tectonic plates grind past each other. The San Andreas. The Anatolian. The plates move centimeters per year, but the friction locks them. Stress builds. Years, decades, centuries of stored energy. Then — release. Minutes of destruction that reshape landscapes, topple cities, rewrite geography.

**Dimensions**:
```

scale: continental (plate boundaries) + local (the rupture zone)
temporal: deep time (plates always moving) + instant (earthquake)
perceptual: undetectable (stress invisible) until catastrophic
relational: non-human (tectonic, geological)
agential: planetary (forces far beyond human scale)
structural: boundary-only (plate boundary, fracture zone)
```
