# The Ritual Edge

**Practice**: Sociology
**Operation**: `Sequence(BETRAYAL, FORGIVENESS)`
**Constraint**: The site *is* this trajectory. You cannot inhabit the state without the movement.

---

## State Space

You are in **the necessary fall** — trust requires a break to exist.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Step 1 | Push | You must endanger the other |
| Step 2 | Catch | You must save them |
| Result | Bond | Only formed after Step 2 |
| Failure | Death | If Step 2 fails |

**Tension**: Mechanical (The gears of the ritual must turn).

---

## Action Set

In **Sociology** practice, moves are strictly ordered:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **1. Push** | *commit* | Initiate the danger | Step 2 might fail |
| **2. Catch** | *resolve* | Restore safety | Requires perfect timing |
| **3. Bind** | *connect* | The resulting scar | Permanence |
| **Abort** | *block* | Stop at Step 1 | Murder (accidental) |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Skip to Bond* — trust unearned
- *Stay Safe* — no site exists there
- *Hesitate* — destroys ratio of Push/Catch
