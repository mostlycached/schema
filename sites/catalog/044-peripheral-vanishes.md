# The Thing at Peripheral Vision That Vanishes When Looked At

## The Site

You see something — a movement, a shape — at the edge of your vision. You turn to look. It's gone. Or it was never what you thought. The peripheral perceives differently: more sensitive to motion, less to detail. Some things exist only for the corner of the eye.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: paradoxical (perception-dependent appearance)
scale: momentary (exists only in the glance)
temporal: instant (vanishes upon attention)
perceptual: peripheral-only (direct gaze destroys it)
relational: solitary (only you see it, barely)
agential: anyone (with peripheral vision)
structural: boundary-only (exists only at edge of perception)
```
