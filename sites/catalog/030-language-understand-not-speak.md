# The Language You Understand But Cannot Speak

**Practice**: Contemplation

---

## State Space

You are in **asymmetric competence** — comprehension without production.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Understanding | Complete | You get every word |
| Production | BLOCKED | Words won't come |
| Gap | Structural | Passive ≠ active |

**Tension**: Medium (frustration, grief).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are competence operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Accept** | *hold* | Live with the asymmetry | Permanent state |
| **Analyze** | *focus* | Why is production blocked? | May not resolve |
| **Generalize** | *notice* | All passive/active splits | Goes abstract |
| **Compensate** | *release* | Communicate otherwise (gesture, reply in other language) | Workaround |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Speak* — that's what's blocked
- *Complete* — can't collapse passive into active

---

## Global Operations

| Operation | Contemplative Consequence |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **GENERATE** | Produces: "reading without writing", "recognizing without naming", "heritage speakers" |
| **TRANSFER** | Maps to: heritage (ancestral half-loss), exile (preserved comprehension), child (asymmetric bilingual), recognition (knowing without naming) |
| **DISSOLVE** | Via practice (developing active from passive), immersion, or loss of passive too |
| **EMERGE** | A nod, a gesture, or the shame of silence |

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Accept
```
Understand → can't speak → accept → live in asymmetry
```
**Payoff**: Stability; the gap as given.

### Path B: Analyze
```
Understand → can't speak → "why?" → practice, age, shame?
```
**Payoff**: Understanding the mechanism; may not resolve.

### Path C: Compensate
```
Understand → can't speak → communicate otherwise → gestures, other language
```
**Payoff**: Workaround; communication preserved.

### Path D: Abstract
```
Understand → can't speak → all passive/active splits → generalize
```
**Payoff**: This instance as pattern.

What you hold:   Full comprehension + production failure + grief or frustration
