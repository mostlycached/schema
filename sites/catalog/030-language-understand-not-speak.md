# The Language You Understand But Cannot Speak

## The Site

They're speaking. You understand every word. But when you open your mouth to respond, the words won't come — not in that language. Comprehension without production. The gap between receiving and generating.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (between passive and active)
scale: extended (the asymmetry persists)
temporal: oscillation (understand → try to speak → fail → understand)
perceptual: direct + failure-mediated
relational: dyadic (but asymmetric)
agential: specific-capacity (passive competence)
structural: paradoxical (knowing without doing)
```
