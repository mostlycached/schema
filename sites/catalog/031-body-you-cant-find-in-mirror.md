# The Part of Your Body You Can't Find in the Mirror

## The Site

You see your face. You see your torso. But there are parts of you — the back of your head, the space between your shoulder blades — that you cannot see directly. They exist. They're yours. You'll never see them with your own eyes.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (perceptual self-access)
scale: lifetime (the invisibility is permanent)
temporal: extended (always true)
perceptual: somatic (felt) + absent (unseen)
relational: solitary (only others see what you can't)
agential: anyone (embodied beings)
structural: simple (but profound)
```
