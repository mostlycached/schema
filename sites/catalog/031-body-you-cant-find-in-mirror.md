# The Part of Your Body You Can't Find in the Mirror

**Practice**: Contemplation

---

## State Space

You are at **the structural blind spot** — owning what you can't see.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Existence | Real | These parts exist |
| Ownership | Yours | They're your body |
| Vision | BLOCKED | You'll never see them directly |

**Tension**: Low-medium (philosophical, not urgent).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are self-perception operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Accept** | *hold* | Accept the structural limit | Permanent state |
| **Abstract** | *notice* | All self-invisible parts | Goes general |
| **Bypass** | *release* | Use mirrors, camera, others | Technological workaround |
| **Extend** | *expand* | Internal organs, unconscious | Maximum invisibility |

**Blocked moves**:
- *See directly* — impossible by structure
- *Complete* — the limit is permanent

```
Body → unseeable parts → accept limit → structural perspective constraint

### Path B: Bypass

```
**Payoff**: See, but never directly; always through apparatus.
