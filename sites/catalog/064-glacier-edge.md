# The Glacier's Edge

**Practice**: Contemplation

---

## State Space

You are at **visible deep time** — centuries of snow meeting the present.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Time scale | Centuries | Old ice meeting now |
| Visibility | Direct | You can see deep time |
| Urgency | Increasing | Climate change accelerates |

**Tension**: Low-medium (sublime, not urgent).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are witnessing operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Witness** | *hold* | Watch the process | Deep time visible |
| **Age** | *focus* | "When did this fall as snow?" | Centuries ago |
| **Abstract** | *notice* | All glaciers | Goes global |
| **Project** | *attend* | What happens as they melt? | Tragic |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Engage* — process exceeds human action
- *Complete* — deep time continues
