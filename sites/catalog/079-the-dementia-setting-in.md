# The Dementia Setting In

## The Site

You notice yourself forgetting. Not the normal forgetting — the pattern. Words that won't come. Routes that confuse. Faces that don't resolve. And the worst: knowing you're forgetting, watching yourself dissolve. The self that notices is the self that's going. You are watching yourself disappear.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: process (dissolution of self)
scale: extended → lifetime (months, years of decline)
temporal: accumulation (progressive, irreversible)
perceptual: failure-mediated (you know through what you can't do)
relational: solitary (you alone in the dissolving)
agential: losing (agency draining away)
structural: recursive (the self watches the self fail)

// Sanitization-resistant dimensions
cleanliness: n/a (not about cleanliness)
dignity: degraded → contemptible (you will become helpless)
redemption: irredeemable (there is no cure)
meaning: anti-meaning (the meaning-maker is dissolving)
aesthetic: pathetic (the reduced self)
```

## The Phenomenology

### The Early Signs
```
- Word-finding (the word is there... no it's not)
- Names (who is that? you knew them)
- Routes (where am I going? why am I here?)
- Tasks (what was I doing?)
- The meta-awareness (I'm forgetting more than I used to)
```

### The Knowing
```
- Comparison to before (I used to be able to...)
- Hiding it (covering for mistakes, making excuses)
- Testing yourself (can I still remember...?)
- The dread (this is going to get worse)
- The identity question (who will I be when I can't remember who I was?)
```

### The Progression
```
- More forgotten than remembered
- Faces that should be familiar
- Your own history becoming foreign
- The self that remembers shrinking
- And then: not knowing that you don't know
- And then: not you at all
```

## The Recursive Horror

The specific horror of dementia:
1. **The self watches itself go** — at least at first
2. **The watcher is also dissolving** — the meta-level fails too
3. **You know you're losing** — until you don't know
4. **Not-knowing is the end** — when you can't notice, you're gone
5. **The body remains** — the person doesn't

---

## What's Revealed

When the self dissolves:

1. **Self is memory** — without memory, who are you?
2. **Identity is continuity** — break the thread, lose the self
3. **The body outlives the person** — you can still breathe but not be
4. **Dignity is conditional** — it can be stripped by biology
5. **There is no final wisdom** — you don't "learn" from this, you just go

## The Unredeemed

This site cannot be redeemed:
- There is no cure
- The dissolution continues
- You lose the capacity to make meaning
- The meaning-maker dissolves
- What remains is not you

What the sanitization-resistant schema must hold: **the self that watches itself disappear, with no redemption arc**.
