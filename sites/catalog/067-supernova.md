# The Supernova

## The Site

A star dies. In seconds, it releases more energy than our Sun will produce in its entire lifetime. For weeks, it outshines its entire galaxy. The explosion creates and distributes heavy elements — the iron in your blood, the calcium in your bones was forged in a supernova. The death of a star seeds the birth of worlds. The ultimate cosmic paradox: destruction that is creation.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: process (stellar death/cosmic creation)
scale: cosmic (galaxy-outshining, element-creating)
temporal: seconds (core collapse) + weeks (visibility) + billions of years (aftermath)
perceptual: distant (too far to witness directly from Earth) or overwhelming (if close)
relational: non-human (stellar, cosmic)
agential: none (stars aren't agents) + all (we are made of this)
structural: paradoxical (destruction that creates)
```
