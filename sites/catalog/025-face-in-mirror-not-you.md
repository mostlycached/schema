# The Face in the Mirror That Isn't You

## The Site

You look at your reflection. You know it's you. But for an instant — or longer — the face looking back is a *stranger's* face. Familiar features, unfamiliar person. You've fallen into the gap between self and image.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: relation (self→image collapse)
scale: momentary → extended (can persist, recur)
temporal: instant (the recognition failure)
perceptual: direct (but the directness is the problem)
relational: solitary (but doubled)
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (you and not-you simultaneously)
```

## Flux Relation

```
flux_state: oscillating (self/not-self)
dissolution_resistance: low — recognition usually returns
hover_potential: medium — can maintain the split briefly
emergence_pattern: perceptual (triggered by staring, angle, state)
flux_pockets: which one is "really" you is flux; both exist
```

This is a paradigmatic uncanny valley site. The self is both present (you're looking at it) and absent (it's a stranger). The oscillation IS the site — not a failure to reach stable ground, but the revelation that recognition is constructed.
