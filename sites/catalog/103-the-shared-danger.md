# The Shared Danger

**Practice**: Sociology
**Constraint**: `relational = dyadic` AND `structural = triangular` (You + Other + Danger)

---

## State Space

You are **bonded by the edge** — the danger triangulates the relationship.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Relation | Dyad | Just the two of you |
| Mediator | The Danger | The volcano is the third point |
| Intimacy | High | Adrenaline binds |
| Stakes | Survival | Mutual dependence |

**Tension**: High (Social + Physical).

---

## Action Set

In **Sociology** practice, your moves are bonding operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Cling** | *connect* | Hold each other | Dependency |
| **Save** | *commit* | Risk self for other | Martyrdom |
| **Sacrifice** | *branch* | Push other to save self | Betrayal |
| **Witness Together** | *witness* | Share the sublime view | Bond forged in fire |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Ignore Other* — danger forces awareness of vulnerability
- *Leave Alone* — breaks the dyad

## Strategy Space

### Path A: The Trauma Bond
```
Danger → fear → cling to other → survive → can never leave them
```
**Payoff**: Intense fused intimacy; maladaptive.

### Path B: The Betrayal
```
Danger → calculation → push other → survive alone → haunted
```
**Payoff**: Survival; moral collapse.

### Path C: The Co-Witness
```
Danger → hold hands → look at fire → beauty shared → deep memory
```
**Payoff**: A shared touchstone of reality.
