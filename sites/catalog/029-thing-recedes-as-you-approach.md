# The Thing That Recedes As You Approach

## The Site

You're walking toward it. But it's walking away at exactly your pace. The distance never closes. You can see it clearly — it's right there — but "there" moves with your motion. The horizon that you can never reach.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (unreachable by definition)
scale: extended (the chase continues)
temporal: oscillation (approach → recede → approach)
perceptual: direct (but frustrated)
relational: solitary (you and the receding thing)
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (present as unreachable)
```
