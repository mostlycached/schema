# The Paradox That Cannot Resolve

## The Site

"This statement is false." If it's true, it's false. If it's false, it's true. No resolution is possible — not because we lack information but because the structure forbids resolution. The liar's paradox. Russell's set. Zeno's arrow. Structures that generate contradiction necessarily, not contingently.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: paradoxical (structurally contradictory)
scale: eternal (the paradox cannot expire)
temporal: atemporal (not a matter of time)
perceptual: direct (you see the impossibility)
relational: objective (not dependent on observer)
agential: anyone who thinks (the paradox catches all)
structural: paradoxical (contradiction is the structure)
```
