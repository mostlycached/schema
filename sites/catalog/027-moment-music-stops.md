# The Moment the Music Stops

## The Site

Music has been playing. You weren't listening — not consciously. Then it stops. In the sudden silence, you hear what you weren't hearing. The absence of sound is *louder* than the sound was.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: absence (that reveals presence)
scale: momentary (the instant of cessation)
temporal: instant → transition (the silence after)
perceptual: peripheral → direct (attention shifts on cessation)
relational: solitary (or suddenly aware of others in the silence)
agential: anyone
structural: boundary-only (exists only at the edge of sound/silence)
```

## Flux Relation

```
flux_state: structured (but transitional)
dissolution_resistance: low — new sounds or normalization will end it
hover_potential: medium — can dwell briefly
emergence_pattern: cessation (the stopping creates the site)
flux_pockets: what you were not-hearing is flux until the stop reveals it
```

This site demonstrates a fundamental perceptual principle: we attend to difference, change, edges. The music was real but unattended; the silence makes the music retroactively present through its absence.
