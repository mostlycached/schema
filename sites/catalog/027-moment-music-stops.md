# The Moment the Music Stops

**Practice**: Music

---

## State Space

You are at **a rest that reveals the preceding phrase** — silence as the articulation point.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Ontology | Absence | The sound is gone; the silence is shaped by it |
| Temporal | Boundary | Exists only at the edge of sound/silence |
| Perception | Inverted | What was background becomes foreground |

**Tension**: Medium. The silence is *louder* than the sound was.

---

## Action Set

In **Music** practice, your moves are compositional operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Rest** | *sustain silence* | Hold the fermata; let resonance decay | May normalize |
| **Develop theme** | *narrow* | What was playing? Reconstruct the absent motif | Loses the silence |
| **Modulate** | *widen* | Connect to all unattended sounds | Goes abstract |
| **Vary** | *branch* | Listen for what emerges in the silence | New material |
| **Pedal point** | *suspend* | Dwell on the cessation as tonic | Contemplative |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Cadence* — there's no resolution; the phrase is cut
- *Arrive* — you can't make the music return by force

---

## Payoff Matrix

| Action | Immediate Payoff | Long-term Consequence |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Rest → dwell in silence** | +uncanny depth | Hear the shape of what's missing |
| **Narrow → reconstruct sound** | +memory | What WAS that melody? |
| **Narrow → texture of silence** | +novelty | "What does this silence sound like?" |
| **Widen → all background sounds** | +scope | What else is constantly playing, unheard? |
| **Widen → all unattended perception** | +vertigo | "What else am I missing?" |
| **Transfer → ×figure/ground** | +inversion | Background becomes foreground |
| **Transfer → ×death** | +tragic depth | "We notice when it's gone" |

| Operation | Musical Consequence |

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Dwell in fermata
```
Sound stops → hold silence → let resonance decay → silence has shape
```
**Payoff**: The rest becomes the phrase. Absence as music.

### Path B: Reconstruct the melody
```
Sound stops → "what was playing?" → memory reconstructs → retroactive attention
```
**Payoff**: The past sound becomes vivid through absence.

### Path C: Escalate to all perception
```
Sound stops → "what else?" → all background sounds → "what else am I missing?"
```
**Payoff**: Maximum vertigo. Perception revealed as partial.

### Path D: Death transfer (tragic)
```
Sound stops → ×death → "presence by cessation" → we notice when it's gone
```
**Payoff**: Existential depth. Sound as life; silence as loss.
