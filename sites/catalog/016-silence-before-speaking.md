# The Silence Before Someone Speaks

## The Site

A room full of people. A question has been asked, or a crisis named, or a truth spoken. Now: silence. Everyone knows someone should speak. The silence *presses*. It's waiting to be broken.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: tension (social)
scale: momentary (but feels longer)
temporal: instant (stretched)
perceptual: peripheral (you sense others sensing it)
relational: collective
agential: anyone (but someone specific must act)
structural: boundary-only (exists only as threshold before speech)
```

---
