# The Mother's Body

## The Site

The body you came from. The flesh that made you, held you, expelled you. The breast you fed from. The smell you know before you know anything. To become a subject, Kristeva says, you had to reject this body — the primal site of your dependence. But it's still there: the abjection at the origin. The body you needed completely, that you must push away to become yourself.

## The Phenomenology

### The Primal Body
```
- The womb (you were inside her, not separate)
- The birth (you were expelled, violently separated)
- The breast (you fed from her body, literally)
- The skin (you were held against it, merged)
- The smell (you knew it before you knew anything)
```

### The Abjection (Kristeva)
```
- To become a subject, you must separate
- To separate, you must reject what you were merged with
- The mother's body becomes abject: too close, threatening
- The abject is not object (outside) but what was once undifferentiated
- Horror and fascination: the body you came from
```

### The Return
```
- In dreams (the primal merge returns)
- In intimacy (merger sought and feared)
- In the mother's aging (her body decaying, prefiguring yours)
- In birth (if you birth, you become the abject body)
- In death (return to undifferentiation)
```

## Kristeva's Theory

From *Powers of Horror*:

> "Abjection preserves what existed in the archaism of pre-objectal relationship, in the immemorial violence with which a body becomes separated from another body in order to be."

The mother's body is abject because:
1. **It's the origin** — before you were separate
2. **You must reject it** — to become a self
3. **It threatens your boundaries** — too close, merger
4. **It's neither self nor other** — the undifferentiated
5. **It returns** — in dreams, in intimacy, in horror

## The Unredeemed

This site is not about redemption:
- The primal relation is primal — before morality
- The abjection is structural — necessary for subjectivity
- The mother's body just is — origin, matter, flesh
- There's no "getting past" it — it returns

What the sanitization-resistant schema must hold: **the abject origin, the flesh you came from, the body you must reject**.
