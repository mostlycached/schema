## Reading the Catalog

Each entry demonstrates:

1. **Gesture Profile**: Available, blocked, dominant, and latent gestures at the site.
2. **Gesture Transitions**: TransitionValue for each gesture — the movement's mode, affect, values, and frame displaced. This is the key structure: TransitionValue belongs to *gestures executed from the site*, not to the site itself.
3. **Global Gestures**: GENERATE, SPECULATE, TRANSFER, DISSOLVE, EMERGE operations and their outcomes.
4. **Flux Relation**: The site's relationship to structure/flux dynamics — flux_state, dissolution_resistance, hover_potential, emergence_pattern, flux_pockets.
5. **Move Notation**: How we got here. Reproducible sequence.
6. **Branch Tree**: What alternatives exist. The paths *not* taken.
7. **State Dynamics**: What happens next. What unlocks, what triggers.
8. **Transfers Applied**: How strange lenses produce vertigo.

The goal is **mobility**: showing that any site could be otherwise, and that moving through the system generates new sites. Each gesture from a site carries a TransitionValue that evaluates the *movement*, not the destination.
