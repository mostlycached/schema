# The Skill You Have But Cannot Teach

## The Site

You can do it. You've done it a thousand times. But when someone asks you to explain how, you open your mouth and nothing useful comes out. The skill lives in your body, not your words. It's yours but not your's to give.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (between tacit and explicit)
scale: extended (the skill persists; the teaching fails)
temporal: oscillation (you do it, you try to explain, you do it again)
perceptual: somatic + failure-mediated (the failure reveals the gap)
relational: dyadic (teacher-student) but broken
agential: expert
structural: paradoxical (possessed but untransmittable)
```

This site marks a fundamental limit: not all knowledge is propositional. The body knows things the mind cannot speak. The frame displaced: knowledge as shareable, transmittable, articulable.
