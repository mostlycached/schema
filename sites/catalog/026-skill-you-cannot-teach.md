# The Skill You Have But Cannot Teach

**Practice**: Design

---

## State Space

You are at **the tacit/explicit boundary** — the limit of articulation.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Skill | Possessed | You can do it |
| Words | BLOCKED | No adequate description |
| Transmission | Failed | Gap is in the medium, not the student |

**Tension**: Medium-high. Frustration at limit.

---

## Action Set

In **Design** practice, your moves are transmission attempts:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Try again** | *iterate* | Another explanation | May fail again |
| **Demonstrate** | *prototype* | "Just watch me" | Not the same as teaching |
| **Analyze** | *narrow* | Isolate the component | Skill may resist decomposition |
| **Accept** | *suspend* | This can't be taught in words | Give up on verbal route |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Complete* — can't resolve tacit into explicit

## Global Operations

| Operation | Design Consequence |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **GENERATE** | Produces: "riding a bike", "wine tasting", "recognizing faces", "hitting a baseball" |
| **TRANSFER** | Maps to: bicycle (balance), wine (perception), embodiment (body-knowledge), transmission (signal loss) |
| **DISSOLVE** | Doesn't dissolve — student either suddenly gets it or doesn't |
| **PARASITIZE** | The Checklist intercepts the tacit; reducing the master's art to a dead procedure that kills the spirit |
| **EMERGE** | A master (who cannot explain), a failed student, or a mystery |

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Try again
```
Fail to teach → another explanation → may still fail
```
**Payoff**: Iteration; low probability of success.

### Path B: Demonstrate
```
Fail to explain → "watch me" → student observes → must decode themselves
```
**Payoff**: Bypasses words; puts burden on student.

### Path C: Analyze
```
Fail to explain → "what exactly is the skill?" → decompose → may resist decomposition
```
**Payoff**: May find articulation route; probably not.

### Path D: Accept the limit
```
Fail to explain → accept → "you'll get it or you won't" → surrender to tacit
```
**Payoff**: Peace with impossibility.

## Position Summary

```
Where you are:   At the tacit/explicit boundary; limit of articulation
What you hold:   Skill you can do + inability to transmit in words
What blocks you: Gap is structural; not all knowledge is propositional
Best moves:      Demonstrate (show, don't tell) or Accept (surrender)
Dominant affect: Tragic — you can't give what you have
```
