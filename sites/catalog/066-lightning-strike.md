# The Lightning Strike

## The Site

30,000°C — five times hotter than the sun's surface. One billion joules of energy. 30 microseconds. The air itself becomes plasma, expanding so fast it creates a shockwave: thunder. The flash is the least of it. In that fraction of a second, electrical potential that built over minutes or hours equalizes — violently. The most concentrated release of atmospheric energy.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: process (instantaneous energy release)
scale: momentary (30 microseconds) + momentarily visible (kilometers)
temporal: instant (but preceded by buildup, followed by silence)
perceptual: overwhelming (light, sound, ionization)
relational: non-human (atmospheric, electrical)
agential: victim or witness (terror either way)
structural: discharge (potential equalizing)
```
