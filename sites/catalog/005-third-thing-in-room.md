# The Third Thing in the Room

## The Site

Two people in a room. But there's something else present — not either of them, not the furniture. Something that emerges *between* them. A charge. A history. An unspoken agreement about what they're doing here.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: relation
scale: extended
temporal: oscillation (appears, recedes, appears)
perceptual: peripheral (can't look at it directly)
relational: dyadic → triadic (the third is the relation itself)
agential: anyone
structural: boundary-only (exists only at the interface)
```
