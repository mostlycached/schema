# The Present Instant

## The Site

Now. This moment. But when you try to grasp it, it's already gone. The present has no duration — any interval you can name includes past and future. Yet everything happens "now." The present is the knife-edge between what was and what will be, infinitely thin, yet the only place anything actually exists.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: paradoxical (real but durationless)
scale: zero (no width) yet infinite (everything happens here)
temporal: pure presence (neither past nor future)
perceptual: immediate (always experienced) yet ungraspable (cannot isolate)
relational: universal (everything shares the now)
agential: anyone (all experience happens now)
structural: boundary-only (the boundary between past and future)
```
