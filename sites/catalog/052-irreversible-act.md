# The Irreversible Act

## The Site

Pulling the trigger. Saying "I do." Pressing send on the resignation letter. The birth. The death you cause. Some acts cannot be undone. Time has many arrows but none that point backward here. The moment after the irreversible act — you cannot return to who you were before it.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (temporal irreversibility)
scale: instant (the act) → lifetime (the consequence)
temporal: one-way (no return)
perceptual: direct (you watch yourself do it) + retrospective (you see what you've done)
relational: varies (may affect only self, may affect many)
agential: actor (the one who does)
structural: complete (fully actualized)
```
