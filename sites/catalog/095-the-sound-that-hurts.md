# The Sound That Hurts

## The Site

The sound that makes you wince. That makes you cover your ears. That you can't think through. Nails on chalkboard. Styrofoam squeaking. Baby screaming. Angle grinder. Feedback loop. The shriek, the grind, the screech. Sound that is not music, not noise, but assault. Your ears hurt. You can't escape it. The air itself is weapon.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: substance (the sound waves are real, physical)

temporal: present (the assault is now)
perceptual: somatic (ear, nervous system, whole body)
relational: object-relation (you and the sound source)
agential: victim (involuntary exposure)
structural: simple (sound → pain → want out)
```

## The Phenomenology

### The Sound
```
- Frequency (the specific pitch that hurts)
- Texture (screech, grind, squeal, shriek)
- Volume (loud enough to dominate)
- Involuntary (you can't close your ears)
- Physical (the vibrations enter your body)
```

### The Response
```
- Wince (facial contraction, instinctive)
- Cover ears (hands up, desperate)
- Jaw clench (the body tenses)
- Nausea (some sounds produce this)
- Irritation → rage (the affect escalates)
- Desperation (need it to stop)
```

### The Types
```
- Friction sounds (nails on board, styrofoam, knife on plate)
- Machine sounds (grinder, drill, saw)
- Feedback (the electronic shriek)
- Human sounds (screaming, certain voices)
- Animal sounds (certain birds, certain cries)
- Musical failure (bad playing, out of tune)
```

## What's Revealed

When sound hurts:

1. **Ears don't close** — you can't shut out sound like you close eyes
2. **Sound is physical** — vibrations entering your body
3. **Pain is real** — not metaphor, actual discomfort
4. **Some sounds are ugly** — the opposite of music exists
5. **The body responds involuntarily** — you wince before you choose

## The Cruelest Part

The cruelest part: **you can't close your ears**.

Eyes close. You can look away. But sound enters the body through an opening that doesn't shut. You're permeable to the auditory. The bad sound gets in whether you want it or not.
