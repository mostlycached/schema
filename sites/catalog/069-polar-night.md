# The Polar Night

## The Site

The sun does not rise. For days. Weeks. Months. At the poles, darkness lasts from September to March or March to September. Not just night — the absence of day. The sky may lighten to twilight at noon, then fade. Stars wheel but the sun never appears. Time loses its grip when the ancient cycle of light and dark ceases.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (temporal/perceptual)
scale: months (duration) + planetary (axial tilt creates this)
temporal: suspended (day/night distinction collapses)
perceptual: darkness (with gradients) + disorientation
relational: solitary (isolation amplified) or collective (polar communities)
agential: inhabitant (enduring)
structural: cyclical (predictable, inevitable) + boundary (latitude determines)
```
