# The Warmth of a Recently Vacated Seat

## The Site

You sit down. The seat is warm. Someone was just here. Their body heat persists in matter they've left. You're in contact with a trace of their presence — intimate, involuntary, strange.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: absence (presence-as-residue)
scale: momentary (the warmth fades quickly)
temporal: transition (presence → absence → trace)
perceptual: somatic (felt in body)
relational: dyadic (you and the absent one) + hybrid (body-matter-body)
agential: anyone
structural: boundary-only (exists only in the transition)
```
