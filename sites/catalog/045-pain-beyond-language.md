# The Pain Beyond Language

## The Site

Pain that cannot be described. Not "sharp" or "dull" or "throbbing" — beyond the words. The language of pain was developed for common pains. But this pain has no words. You point at it, cry out, but the sound isn't description. The pain is perfectly real and perfectly unsayable.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (language's edge)
scale: varies (but felt as infinite)
temporal: extended (the unsayable persists)
perceptual: somatic (utterly felt) + absent (from language)
relational: solitary (incommunicable)
agential: sufferer (the one who can't say)
structural: boundary-only (where language stops)
```
