# The Experience Only Dying People Have

## The Site

They report strange things. The tunnel of light. The life review. The presence of dead relatives. The overwhelming peace. Or the opposite: terror, isolation, the void. Whatever it is, you can't have it and report back fully. Those who return weren't quite dead; those who don't return never tell. The experience at the boundary of existence.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (life/death boundary)
scale: momentary (the dying) + eternal (what comes after?)
temporal: terminal (the last experience)
perceptual: unknown (we have only reports from returns)
relational: solitary (you die alone) + possibly relational (the presences some report)
agential: dying (the one leaving)
structural: boundary-only (accessible only at the edge)
```
