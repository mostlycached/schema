# The Event Horizon

## The Site

The point of no return. Cross it and nothing — not light, not information, not time as we know it — comes back. The black hole's boundary isn't a surface but a mathematical limit: inside, all worldlines lead to the singularity. The ultimate one-way door. From outside, you'd see things falling in slow forever, reddening into invisibility. From inside? No "inside" is observable.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (absolute causal boundary)
scale: cosmic (stellar masses, galactic centers)
temporal: paradoxical (infinite from outside, finite from inside)
perceptual: impossible (nothing returns to report)
relational: non-human (gravitational, physical)
agential: none possible (no observers survive meaningfully)
structural: boundary-only (pure limit, no interior access)
```
