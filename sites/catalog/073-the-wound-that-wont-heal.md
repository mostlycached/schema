# The Wound That Won't Heal

## The Site

You have a wound. It's been weeks. Months. It weeps. It crusts over and then cracks open. You tend it — clean, dress, protect — but it doesn't close. The body fails its basic contract: damage should repair. Instead: chronic ooze, the daily tending, the smell of infection or just of flesh that won't knit.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: process (failed repair)
scale: extended (weeks, months, indefinite)
temporal: oscillation (better, worse, better, worse)
perceptual: somatic (pain, itch, wetness, awareness of breach)
relational: solitary (you and your wound)
agential: patient (tending, not healing)
structural: recursive (wound → tend → wound → tend)

// Sanitization-resistant dimensions
cleanliness: soiled (ooze, crust, smell)
dignity: diminished (the body's failure visible)
redemption: unredeemed (no healing, no lesson)
meaning: meaning-absent (just failing flesh)
aesthetic: repulsive (wound aesthetics)
```

## The Phenomenology

### The Wound Itself
```
- Open tissue (what should be inside, showing)
- Wetness (serum, blood, pus — the ooze)
- Crust (the body's failed attempt at closure)
- Edges (skin that won't meet skin)
- Smell (infection, or just open flesh)
- Color (red, yellow, gray — health or rot?)
```

### The Tending
```
- Unwrapping (seeing it again, checking: better or worse?)
- Cleaning (gentle pressure, wincing, removing debris)
- Assessing (is that new tissue? Is that pus? Is it bigger?)
- Dressing (gauze, tape, the ritual of covering)
- Wrapping (hiding it again until tomorrow)
```

### The Living-With
```
- Positioning (avoiding pressure on the wound)
- Awareness (constant low-level knowledge of the breach)
- Smell-checking (do others notice?)
- Fatigue (the body's resources diverted)
- Resignation (it's not going to heal)
```

## What's Revealed

When RESOLVE is blocked:

1. **The body's limits** — it cannot always repair
2. **Chronic vs. acute** — not all damage is temporary
3. **The daily labor** — tending that produces nothing
4. **The ooze as fact** — not metaphor, just serum
5. **Undignified embodiment** — the leaking, failing body
