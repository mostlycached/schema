# The Mediocre Life

## The Site

Your life is fine. Not bad. Not good. Not tragic enough to be interesting. Not successful enough to be admirable. Just: mediocre. Average. The middle of the bell curve. You're not failing. You're not succeeding. You're just... there. Existing. Neither remarkable nor remarkably bad. The life that no one would write about.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: substance (the life is real, lived)
scale: lifetime (the whole thing)
temporal: duration (ongoing, unchanging middle)
perceptual: direct (you experience your mediocrity)
relational: varies (but unremarkable in all relations)
agential: agent (you made choices that led here)
structural: simple (middle, staying middle)

// Sanitization-resistant dimensions
cleanliness: n/a (not about cleanliness)
dignity: ordinary → diminished (the shrinking self)
redemption: n/a (nothing to redeem — just medium)
meaning: meaning-absent (what's the meaning of average?)
aesthetic: banal (the aesthetic of the unremarkable)
```

## What's Revealed

When you face mediocrity:

1. **Most lives are like this** — the middle is crowded
2. **Remarkable is rare** — by definition
3. **You are not special** — statistically accurate
4. **Days add up to nothing much** — that's what mediocre means
5. **There's no drama** — just ongoing okay-ness

## The Unredeemed

This site cannot be redeemed:
- It's not broken (can't be fixed)
- It's not wrong (just average)
- It's not tragic (just unremarkable)
- There's no arc (just plateau)
- The meaning is: there's no special meaning

What the sanitization-resistant schema must hold: **the unremarkable life, without finding hidden value**.

---

## The Cruelest Part

The cruelest part: **it's not bad enough to be interesting**.

If you were suffering greatly, there'd be a story. If you were failing spectacularly, there'd be drama. But mediocrity has no narrative. It just continues, middle-of-the-road, until it stops.
