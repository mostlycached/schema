# Pure Chaos

## The Site

No pattern. No repetition. No law. Every moment utterly disconnected from every other. Even the idea of "moment" implies structure — pure chaos has no time, no space, no relation. Is it even thinkable? We can approach it in thought: maximum entropy, white noise, perfect randomness. But even randomness has statistical properties. Pure chaos would have nothing at all.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: flux (absolute, zero structure)
scale: undefined (no measure possible)
temporal: undefined (no sequence, no duration)
perceptual: impossible (perception requires pattern)
relational: none (no relations without structure)
agential: none (no agents in pure chaos)
structural: none (structure = zero)
```
