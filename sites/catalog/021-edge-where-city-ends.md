# The Edge Where the City Ends

## The Site

There's a line — sometimes sharp, sometimes blurred — where the city gives way to something else. Fields. Wasteland. Suburbs that deny being suburbs. You can stand at the edge and feel the city *stop*.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (spatial)
scale: extended
temporal: transition (historically: ever-shifting)
perceptual: direct
relational: collective (the city's edge belongs to no one)
agential: anyone
structural: boundary-only
```

```

dissolution_resistance: low — edges move historically

emergence_pattern: spatial (reaching the boundary creates the site)
flux_pockets: where exactly the edge is = flux; that there is an edge = structured
```

The edge is a natural hover site. It exists as threshold, not as stable position. Standing at the edge is dwelling in transition — between inside/outside, city/not-city, human/nonhuman.
