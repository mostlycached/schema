# The Word That Loses Meaning When Repeated

## The Site

Say a word fifty times. *Door. Door. Door. Door.* Watch it dissolve. The sounds persist but the meaning drains away. What was solid becomes mere noise. You've broken something that shouldn't break.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: process (dissolution)
scale: momentary → extended (the effect builds)
temporal: accumulation (negative; meaning un-accumulates)
perceptual: direct + somatic (you feel the dissolution)
relational: solitary
agential: anyone
structural: paradoxical (the word is still there; meaning isn't)
```
