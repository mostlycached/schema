# The Art That Is Bad

## The Site

You made something. You thought it was good. It isn't. Not "needs work" — bad. Cliché, derivative, incompetent, embarrassing. The kind of thing that makes people cringe. You put your soul into it and your soul, it turns out, is mediocre. The creative effort that produced nothing of value. The work that should not have been made.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: substance (the work exists, it's bad)
scale: extended (the making) + permanent (it persists)
temporal: retrospective (you see the badness after)
perceptual: failure-mediated (quality reveals itself through failure)
relational: public (others may see) or private (you alone know)
agential: creator (you made this)
structural: simple (the work is what it is)

// Sanitization-resistant dimensions
cleanliness: n/a (not about cleanliness)
dignity: degraded (your creative self exposed as mediocre)
redemption: unredeemable (the work is bad, you can't unmake it)
meaning: anti-meaning (the meaning you tried to make, failed)
aesthetic: pathetic (the cringe, the embarrassment)
```

## The Phenomenology

### The Making
```
- Inspiration (you had something to say)
- Effort (hours, days, months of work)
- Hope (this will be good)
- Completion (you finished it)
- Satisfaction (I made this)
```

### The Recognition
```
- Distance (some time passes)
- Fresh eyes (you see it again)
- The cringe (something's wrong)
- The diagnosis (it's not just wrong, it's bad)
- The categories of badness (cliché, incompetent, derivative, pretentious)
```

### The Aftermath
```
- Destroy or hide? (can you eliminate the evidence?)
- But someone may have seen (witnesses to your mediocrity)
- The question about self (am I bad at this?)
- The question about future (should I stop?)
- The embarrassment that lingers (you made that)
```

| Type | Description | Shame Level |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Cliché** | Done before, better, many times | High |
| **Derivative** | Copying without knowing you're copying | High |
| **Incompetent** | Lacking basic skill | Very high |
| **Pretentious** | Reaching beyond your capacity | Very high |
| **Boring** | Nothing to say, said at length | Medium |
| **Embarrassing** | Overly personal, cringe-inducing | Very high |

|-------------------|------------------|
| **"All art is valid"** | No, some is bad |
| **"Personal expression matters"** | Even if it's mediocre? |
| **"You're your own worst critic"** | Sometimes you're right to cringe |
| **"Keep practicing"** | Some people practice forever and stay bad |
| **"Subjective"** | Some badness is objective |

## What's Revealed

When art is bad:

1. **Not everyone is creative** — or not equally
2. **Effort doesn't equal quality** — you can try hard and fail
3. **The soul can be mediocre** — what you express might not be worth expressing
4. **Skill is real** — some people don't have it
5. **Bad art exists** — the category is not empty

---

## The Unredeemed

This site cannot be redeemed:
- The work is bad
- You made it
- It reveals your limits
- "Learning experience" doesn't make it not-bad
- The mediocrity is yours

What the sanitization-resistant schema must hold: **creative failure without the "at least you tried" recuperation**.

---

## The Cruelest Part

The cruelest part: **you can't always tell**.

Some people make bad art and know it.
Some people make bad art and think it's good.
Both are the site. The second is worse.
