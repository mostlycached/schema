# The Infinite Debt

**Practice**: Economics
**Operation**: `Recursion(PAYMENT → INCREASES_DEBT)`
**Constraint**: A generative loop where the act of resolving the tension explicitly amplifies it. Sisyphus as an economic model.

---

## State Space

You are in **the theological economy** — where the currency is your own substance.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Ledger | Infinite | Total debt exceeds total value of world |
| Currency | Somatic | Paid in tissue, time, and vitality |
| Transaction| Recursive | Interest > Payment |
| Relation | Bondage | You belong to the Creditor |

**Tension**: Crushing (The impossibility of zero).

## Action Set

In **Economics** practice, moves are traps:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Pay** | *commit* | Sacrifice tissue/time | Debt grows (fee for paying) |
| **Audit** | *witness* | Calculate the total | Madness (infinity seen) |
| **Default** | *block* | Refuse to pay | Foreclosure (Death) |
| **Borrow** | *branch* | Take more to pay now | Acceleration of entropy |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Settle* — impossible
- *Save* — currency decays instantly

## Strategy Space

### Path A: The Sinner (Pay)
```
Debt exists → work harder → pay with health → health fails → productivity drops → debt grows
```
**Payoff**: The dignity of the struggle; ultimate failure.

### Path B: The Theologian (Audit)
```
Debt exists → study the ledger → realize it is infinite → worship the debt → submission
```
**Payoff**: Peace through acceptance of slavery.

### Path C: The Accelerationist (Borrow)
```
Debt exists → borrow differently → leverage the debt → crash the system
```
**Payoff**: Catastrophe as liberation.
