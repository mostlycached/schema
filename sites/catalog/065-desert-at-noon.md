# The Desert at Noon

**Practice**: Contemplation

---

## State Space

You are at **maximum exposure** — where the planet tries to kill you with light.

| Dimension | Value | Constraint |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Light | Maximum | No shadows anywhere |
| Temperature | Extreme | 50°C+ |
| Shelter | Zero | Nowhere to hide |

**Tension**: Maximum (survival timeline).

---

## Action Set

In **Contemplation** practice, your moves are endurance operations:

| Move | Verb | Effect | Risk |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **Endure** | *suspend* | Survive until cool | Body fails |
| **Seek** | *branch* | Find water/shade | Exertion kills |
| **Surrender** | *release* | Accept exposure | Death |
| **Abstract** | *widen* | Human survival limits | Goes general |

**Blocked moves**:
- *Hide* — no shade
- *Rest* — heat lethal

---

| Action | Immediate Payoff | Long-term Consequence |

| **Branch → find shade** | +relief | Any shade helps |

## Global Operations

| Operation | Contemplative Consequence |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **GENERATE** | Produces: "emotional desert", "creative drought", "isolation", "wilderness testing" |
| **TRANSFER** | Maps to: exposure (psychological), drought (deprivation), wilderness (spiritual), waste (stripped) |
| **DISSOLVE** | When rain falls (rare), when oasis found |
| **PARASITIZE** | The AC Unit intercepts the exposure; we carry a bubble of 72°F everywhere, denying the planet's heat |
| **EMERGE** | Nightfall, dehydration, or a hallucination |

## Strategy Space

### Path A: Endure
```
Noon → endure heat → wait for evening → survival
```
**Payoff**: The test of endurance.

### Path B: Seek
```
Noon → move → find water/shade → risk exertion
```
**Payoff**: Active survival; high risk.

### Path C: Abstract
```
Noon → exposure → human fragility → spiritual testing
```
**Payoff**: Meaning in the ordeal.

```
Where you are:   At the limit of exposure; nowhere to hide
What you hold:   Fragility + endurance + relentless light
What blocks you: No water; no shade; environment is hostile
Best moves:      Suspend (endure) or Branch (seek shelter)
```
