# The Desert at Noon

## The Site

Sand extends to every horizon. Temperature: 50°C in the shade (but there is no shade). The sun directly overhead eliminates all shadow. No water. No landmarks. No shade. No escape from the light. The air shimmers. Your body loses water faster than thought. This is where the planet tries to kill you with exposure.

**Dimensions**:
```
ontological: limit (survival boundary)
scale: vast (horizon to horizon emptiness)
temporal: cyclical (day/night extremes) + immediate (your survival timeline)
perceptual: overwhelming (light, heat, vastness)
relational: hostile (environment vs. organism)
agential: survivor (struggling against)
structural: simple (featureless) + lethal (nothing to sustain life)
```
