# Chapter 11: Troubleshooting

> **Prerequisites**: Chapters 1-10
>
> **Learning Objectives**: Diagnose common problems. Know which intervention to try for each symptom.

---

## How to Use This Chapter

When something isn't working, find your symptom below. Each entry gives:
- **Symptom**: What you're experiencing
- **Possible causes**: What might be wrong
- **Diagnosis**: How to figure out which cause
- **Interventions**: What to try

---

## "Nothing is Coming"

### Symptom

You apply constraints, try to generate, and... nothing. Blank. The domain feels empty.

### Possible Causes

1. **Barrenness** — The domain has been filtered too heavily
2. **Wrong practice** — Your practice doesn't engage this field
3. **Over-constraint** — Too many restrictions, nothing satisfies them all
4. **Flux avoidance** — Too much structure, no room for emergence

### Diagnosis

- Check barrenness type (familiarity, completion, categorical, exhaustion, status)
- Check practice-field match (does your practice engage the levels present in this field?)
- Count active constraints (more than 3-4 compound constraints may be over-constraining)
- Check flux access (when did you last dissolve/hover/emerge?)

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Barrenness | Apply defamiliarization by type (Chapter 4) |
| Wrong practice | Switch practice, keep field |
| Over-constraint | Remove constraints one by one until generation resumes |
| Flux avoidance | Controlled dissolution (Pattern 7) |

## "Everything Feels the Same"

### Symptom

Sites are generating, but they all feel like variations of one thing. No diversity.

### Possible Causes

1. **Constraint exhaustion** — The constraint has been fully explored
2. **Attractor basin** — You're stuck in one region of site-space
3. **Single dimension** — Only varying one dimension, others fixed
4. **Low fertility constraint** — The constraint only reveals one region

### Diagnosis

- List the last 5 sites generated. What do they have in common?
- Check gesture profiles. Are they all similar?
- Check dimensional distribution. Are you stuck in one ontological/temporal type?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Constraint exhaustion | Negate the constraint, or relax it, or replace it |
| Attractor basin | Map the basin edges, execute WIDEN or BRANCH from edge |
| Single dimension | Add dimensional constraint targeting a DIFFERENT dimension |
| Low fertility | Try a more open constraint, assess fertility before committing |

## "I Can't Move"

### Symptom

You're at a site but all gestures feel blocked. No available moves.

### Possible Causes

1. **Endpoint site** — You've arrived at a terminus (some sites are endpoints)
2. **Over-commitment** — Previous COMMITs have foreclosed options
3. **Unresolved SUSPEND** — Accumulated suspensions creating paralysis
4. **Practice mismatch** — The site can't be engaged by your current practice

### Diagnosis

- Check gesture profile. What's blocked? What's latent?
- Check history. How many COMMITs have you made recently?
- Check suspensions. Are there unresolved SUSPENDs creating obligation-pressure?
- Try engaging with different practice. Does the site open up?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Endpoint site | Backtrack. This site is done. Move to a sibling or parent site. |
| Over-commitment | Controlled dissolution to release some commitments |
| Unresolved SUSPEND | Execute RESOLVE on suspended sites, discharge obligations |
| Practice mismatch | Switch practice, same domain |

## "It Feels Forced"

### Symptom

Sites are generating but they feel arbitrary, mechanical, unmotivated.

### Possible Causes

1. **Arbitrary constraint** — The constraint doesn't arise from the material
2. **Missing valuation** — Nothing matters more than anything else
3. **External obligation** — You're generating for external reasons, not internal
4. **Practice not internalized** — You're executing operations without feeling them

### Diagnosis

- Check constraint origin. Where did this constraint come from? Does it fit?
- Check valuation. What do you actually care about in this domain?
- Check motivation. Why are you doing this? What's at stake for you?
- Check practice depth. Are you *in* the practice or *performing* the practice?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Arbitrary constraint | Derive constraint from material instead of imposing |
| Missing valuation | Ask: "What matters here? What would make this matter?" |
| External obligation | Either find internal stake or pause until you have one |
| Practice not internalized | Spend time in the practice without generating. Just attend. |

## "I Keep Returning to the Same Place"

### Symptom

Different starting points, different constraints, same sites keep appearing.

### Possible Causes

1. **Strong attractor** — Some sites pull everything toward them
2. **Unexamined assumption** — You're carrying a hidden constraint
3. **Comfort zone** — You unconsciously steer toward familiar territory
4. **Practice habit** — Your practice has grooves it tends to follow

### Diagnosis

- Name the attractor. What's the site you keep finding?
- Ask: "What would I never generate?" (reveals hidden constraints)
- Try opposite constraint. If you resist, you've found the assumption.
- Have someone else look. What do they see that you don't?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Strong attractor | Study the attractor. What makes it attractive? Negate that property. |
| Unexamined assumption | Make the assumption explicit. Then negate it as constraint. |
| Comfort zone | Deliberately generate in the uncomfortable zone. |
| Practice habit | Switch practice temporarily. Return with fresh eyes. |

## "I Don't Know What Domain I'm In"

### Symptom

Confusion about what you're doing. Unable to generate because unclear what field you're engaging or what practice you're using.

### Possible Causes

1. **Unbounded field** — The field isn't constituted
2. **Practice blur** — Multiple practices mixed without clarity
3. **Domain drift** — You've gradually moved away from where you started
4. **Flux state** — You're in flux without realizing it

### Diagnosis

- Can you state the field in one sentence?
- Can you name the practice?
- Trace history: where did you start? How did you get here?
- Check structure: are boundaries and differentiations clear?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Unbounded field | Stop. Apply BOUND. Define the field before proceeding. |
| Practice blur | Choose one practice. Work with it clearly. Add others later if needed. |
| Domain drift | Acknowledge the new domain. Re-enter with fresh diagnosis. |
| Flux state | Recognize you're in flux. Either EMERGE deliberately or continue hovering. |

## "The Constraint Kills Everything"

### Symptom

Under this constraint, no sites satisfy. Generation produces nothing.

### Possible Causes

1. **Impossible constraint** — No site can satisfy (logically impossible)
2. **Too narrow** — Constraint is too specific
3. **Wrong dimension** — Targeting a dimension not present in this field
4. **Contradictory constraints** — Multiple active constraints that conflict

### Diagnosis

- Try to name one site that would satisfy. Can you?
- Check constraint specificity. Is it targeting one exact site?
- Check constraint dimensions against field levels. Match?
- List active constraints. Do any pairs contradict?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Impossible constraint | Relax until possible. Constraints aren't ideals; they're generators. |
| Too narrow | Generalize the constraint. Remove one specification. |
| Wrong dimension | Check field-practice alignment. Adjust constraint to target engaged levels. |
| Contradictory constraints | Resolve contradiction: choose one, or find meta-constraint that encompasses both |

## "I Can't Resolve"

### Symptom

You're at a suspended site, RESOLVE feels necessary, but it's blocked.

### Possible Causes

1. **Intrinsically irresoluble** — Some sites resist resolution by nature
2. **Premature resolution** — Not yet time to resolve
3. **Wrong resolution** — The expected resolution isn't the right one
4. **External resolution needed** — Resolution requires something outside the domain

### Diagnosis

- Is this site paradoxical, oscillating, or incomplete? (These resist RESOLVE)
- Has enough happened since SUSPEND? (Resolution needs development)
- Is the expected resolution the only option? (Maybe there's another)
- Does resolution require information or experience you don't have?

### Interventions

| Cause | Intervention |
|-------|--------------|
| Intrinsically irresoluble | Accept. Let the site remain open. Not everything resolves. |
| Premature resolution | Continue work. Let more develop. Return to resolution later. |
| Wrong resolution | BRANCH before RESOLVE. Find alternative resolutions. |
| External resolution needed | Mark as pending. Exit domain. Gather what's needed. Return. |

---

## Summary

Most problems fall into categories:

- **Generation problems**: Nothing coming, everything same → Check constraints, barrenness
- **Movement problems**: Can't move, keep returning → Check gestures, attractors
- **Orientation problems**: Feels forced, confused → Check MVA, practice clarity
- **Structure problems**: Constraint kills, can't resolve → Check constraint design
