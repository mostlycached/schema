## Why Go Abstract?

We've built up a concrete system: flux, fields, practices, domains, sites, gestures, constraints.

Now we ask: what's underneath? What abstract operations make all of this work?

The answer: **three operations that compose everything**.

---

## The Three Operations

### Mapping

**What it does**: Relates two structures so that something transfers.

**Formal**: M: A → B, where structure in A illuminates or generates structure in B.

Metaphor is one kind of mapping ("love is a journey"). But there are others:
- Analogy (structural similarity)
- Homology (shared origin)
- Isomorphism (exact correspondence)
- Embedding (one inside another)
- Projection (reduction preserving some structure)
- Translation (re-encoding)

**Questions about any mapping**:
- What transfers? Elements? Relations? Operations?
- How does it transfer? Exactly? With loss?
- Which direction? One-way or bidirectional?
- Where does it break?

The failure points are often the most interesting sites.

---

### Valuation

**What it does**: Assigns significance — makes some things matter more than others.

**Formal**: V: X → ℝ, a ranking. Some things higher, some lower.

Narrative significance is one kind of valuation ("the climax matters more than the exposition"). But there are others:
- Preference (I want this more)
- Importance (this matters more)
- Salience (this stands out)
- Stakes (more is at risk)
- Care (I care about this more)

**Questions about any valuation**:
- What's the ground? Why this ranking?
- What type? Cardinal (how much)? Ordinal (just order)? Boolean?
- How stable?
- Is it revisable?

Valuation creates landscape. High-value regions attract. Low-value regions become invisible.

---

### Orientation

**What it does**: Establishes direction — makes some directions more salient.

**Formal**: O: Space → VectorField, associating points with directions.

Telos (purpose, goal) is one kind of orientation. But there are others:
- Origin (where from)
- Destination (where to)
- Gradient (direction of increase)
- Attractor (what pulls)
- Repulsor (what pushes)
- Current (what carries)

**Questions about any orientation**:
- What type? Point attractor? Gradient? Basin?
- What's the source? Purpose? Value? External force?
- How strong?
- How stable?

Orientation creates flow. Some directions feel natural. Others feel upstream.

---

## How They Compose

The three operations are independent but composable:

| Combination | What It Does |
|-------------|--------------|
| M alone | Structure transfers, nothing matters more, no direction |
| V alone | Things matter, no direction |
| O alone | There's direction, nothing matters more |
| M + V | "This is like that, *and it matters the same way*" |
| M + O | "This is like that, *and the direction is the same*" |
| V + O | "This matters, *and there's a direction*" (gradient) |
| **M + V + O** | Full transfer: structure, significance, direction |

**Myth and metaphor** do all three at once. That's why they're powerful generators of meaning.

## Everything is MVA-Structured

### Fields as MVA

A field is:
- **Boundary** (Mapping): Inside/outside distinction
- **Significance** (Valuation): Why this field matters
- **Organization** (Orientation): How attention flows within

### Constraints as MVA

A constraint is:
- **Mapping**: From all possible sites to satisfying sites
- **Valuation**: Satisfaction degree (what satisfies matters more)
- **Orientation**: Toward satisfaction

### Sites as MVA

A site has:
- **Mapping properties**: What it connects to, how structure transfers through it
- **Valuation properties**: Its value (peak? valley?), whether value is contested
- **Orientation properties**: Its directional type (source? sink? path?)

### Gestures as MVA Transforms

Each gesture transforms on all three operations:

| Gesture | Mapping Effect | Valuation Effect | Orientation Effect |
|---------|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| BRANCH | ONE → MANY | Spread value | Create multiple directions |
| NARROW | MANY → FEWER | Concentrate value | Focus direction |
| COMMIT | MANY → ONE | All value to one | Single direction, fixed |
| DEFER | Hold | Preserve | Pause |
| SUSPEND | Create gap (question) | Value the question | Orient toward resolution |
| WIDEN | Extend domain | Extend value | Open new directions |
| RESOLVE | Complete gap | Discharge value | Arrive at attractor |

### Flux as Absence of MVA

Flux is the state where:
- **No mapping**: Nothing relates
- **No valuation**: Nothing matters more
- **No orientation**: No direction

To enter flux is to release M, V, and O.
To emerge from flux is to precipitate all three.

## Generating via MVA

You can generate sites through each operation:

### Via Mapping

Find sites where mappings succeed or fail.
- Where does the analogy hold?
- Where does it break?
- Failure points are sites.

### Via Valuation

Find sites at value peaks, valleys, boundaries.
- What matters most?
- Where is value contested?
- Peaks and contested zones are sites.

### Via Orientation

Find sites at attractors, sources, eddies.
- What's the destination?
- What's the origin?
- Where do currents cross? Those are sites.

### At MVA Convergences

Sites where all three point to the same location are highly charged.
- Mapping says X is connected
- Valuation says X matters
- Orientation points toward X
- X is a convergence site

### At MVA Conflicts

Sites where operations conflict create tension.
- Mapping says X connects
- Valuation says Y matters more
- Orientation points toward Z
- The space between X, Y, Z is a conflict site

## TransitionValue: Evaluating Movement

All movement can be evaluated. Whether you're executing a local gesture (site → site) or a global gesture (generation, speculation, transfer), the transition carries value.

### Two Kinds of Gestures

| Type | What It Moves | Examples |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Local gestures** | Site → Site | BRANCH, NARROW, COMMIT, DEFER, WIDEN... |
| **Global gestures** | Structure → Structure (any level) | GENERATE, SPECULATE, TRANSFER, DISSOLVE, EMERGE |

Global gestures are meta-level operations. Generation produces new structures. Speculation produces under hypotheticals. Transfer applies structure from one domain to another. They're gestures because they're *movements* — they take you from one state to another.

### TransitionValue

Every transition (local or global) has:

| Component | What It Captures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Mode** | Intensity delta: collapse (shrinking/breaking) or expansion (opening/widening) |
| **Affect** | How it feels: comedic, tragic, uncanny, sublime, quotidian, liberating... |
| **Values** | Generative metrics: vertigo, fertility, coherence, novelty, depth, stability |
| **Outcome** | Productivity: productive, decorative, or noise |

### Mode vs Affect

Mode and affect are independent:

| Mode | + Affect | = Experience |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Collapse | Comedic | Bathetic deflation |
| Collapse | Tragic | Loss of ground |
| Collapse | Uncanny | Familiar becoming hostile |
| Expansion | Sublime | Overwhelming opening |
| Expansion | Uncanny | Familiar becoming vast |
| Expansion | Liberating | Constraint dissolving |

The same structural movement (collapse) can feel comedic or tragic. The mode is about *what happened to the frame*. The affect is about *how it felt to be there*.

### Generative Values

Different values matter for different purposes:

| Value | What It Measures | When To Optimize |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Vertigo** | Frame displacement | Breaking out of stuck patterns |
| **Fertility** | Derivation potential | Seeding further work |
| **Coherence** | Fit with existing structures | Building out, extending |
| **Novelty** | Difference from existing | Exploring new territory |
| **Depth** | Levels affected | Systemic impact |
| **Stability** | Persistence expected | Establishing foundations |

You can create profiles: *BreakingOpen* emphasizes vertigo and novelty. *BuildingOut* emphasizes coherence and fertility. *Grounding* emphasizes stability and depth.

### The Vertigo Test

Vertigo is now one value among many. To test for it:

> After the transition, can you still use the naive model innocently?

- **Yes** → Low vertigo (frame survived)
- **No, and structures emerged** → High vertigo, productive
- **No, but nothing emerged** → High vertigo, noise

High vertigo + productive outcome = the transition displaced a frame and generated new structures from the displacement.

### Using TransitionValue

1. **Choose your profile** — What values matter for this work?
2. **Execute transition** — Local or global gesture
3. **Evaluate** — Score against your profile
4. **Harvest** — What precipitated from high-value transitions?

TransitionValue isn't prescriptive. It's diagnostic. You can work without it. But if you want to understand *why* some moves generate and others don't, evaluate the transition.

## Diagnostic Use

Use MVA to diagnose creative problems:

| Symptom | Possible Cause | Intervention |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Work feels disconnected | No mapping | Find structural analogies |
| Work feels flat | No valuation | Ask what matters more |
| Work feels directionless | No orientation | Ask what's pulling |
| Work feels forced | Arbitrary V or O | Check if significance comes from material |
| Work feels ungrounded | Mapping breaking | Find where analogy fails |

## The Full Stack

We can now see the complete system as MVA-structured at every level:

```
Flux (no M, V, O)
  ↓ precipitates
Field (M: boundary, V: significance, O: organization)
  ↓ engaged by Practice
Domain (accumulated MVA structures)
  ↓ under Constraints (M+V+O)
Site (location with M, V, O properties)
  ↓ afford
Gestures (transforms on M, V, O)
```

At every level, the three operations structure what's possible.

## Summary

**Three primitive operations**:
- **Mapping**: Relates structures (A → B)
- **Valuation**: Assigns significance (X → value)
- **Orientation**: Establishes direction (Point → vector)

**They compose**:
- Separately: partial meaning-making
- Together: full transfer (what myth and metaphor do)

**Everything is MVA-structured**:
- Field = Boundary(M) + Significance(V) + Organization(O)
- Constraint = Mapping + Value + Direction
- Site = Location with MVA properties
- Gesture = Transform on MVA
- Flux = Absence of MVA

**Generating via MVA**:
- Via mapping (transfer, failure points)
- Via valuation (peaks, contested zones)
- Via orientation (attractors, eddies)
- At convergences (all three align)
- At conflicts (three pulling differently)

## Exercises

### Warm-up

**8.1** Take a metaphor you use often — "life is a journey," "argument is war," "time is money."

What maps? (M) What values? (V) What directs? (O)

Where does the metaphor break — where does the mapping fail?

**8.2** You're procrastinating on something. Analyze using MVA:
- What's the current valuation? (What matters more than the task?)
- What's the current orientation? (What are you moving toward?)

What would need to change in V or O for the task to happen?

---

### Standard

**8.3** Think of a recent decision you made. Evaluate its TransitionValue:
- Mode: collapse or expansion?
- Affect: what did it feel like? (comedic, tragic, liberating, uncanny...)
- Outcome: productive, decorative, or noise?

Did the decision produce new structures or just move you around in existing ones?

**8.4** Design a "profile" for your current project. Which generative values are you optimizing?
- Vertigo (breaking out)
- Fertility (seeding further work)
- Coherence (fitting with existing)
- Novelty (exploring new territory)
- Stability (establishing foundations)

What would change if you switched to a different profile?

**8.5** The chapter describes MVA conflicts — where mapping says X, valuation says Y, orientation says Z.

Find an MVA conflict in your life. What's pulling in three directions? Is the conflict site fertile or stuck?

---

### Challenge

**8.6** Apply the vertigo test to a belief you hold:

> After examining this belief through a strange lens, can you still use it innocently?

Pick a lens (a practice, a domain, a transfer from a distant field). What happens to the belief?

**8.7** Generation and speculation are described as "global gestures." Local gestures move you between sites; global gestures move you between structures.

What's the TransitionValue of this conversation? What frame has shifted? What structures have precipitated?

Use the vocabulary to analyze what has happened while you've been doing these exercises.
