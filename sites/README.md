## Quick Summary

- **Flux** is the ever-present ground from which structures emerge
- **Fields** are bounded, differentiated regions carved from flux
- **Practices** are modes of engaging with fields
- **Domains** are stabilized practice-field pairings
- **Sites** are positions with embedded gestures, emerging in domains
- **Gestures** are possibility operations (BRANCH, NARROW, COMMIT, DEFER, SUSPEND, WIDEN, RESOLVE)
- **Constraints** are generative restrictions that shape which sites are visible
- **The Three Operations** (Mapping, Valuation, Orientation) are the abstract infrastructure
